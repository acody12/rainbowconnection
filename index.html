THE RAINBOW
CONNECTION

Connecting Chicago Queer Communities.

Chicago’s queer nightlife stretches across the North and West Sides. Getting between neighborhoods late at night can mean long waits or multiple transfers. The Rainbow Connection is a proposed Saturday-night bus line designed to make those trips simpler—and bring more of our community within reach.

FAQs

What to know about the proposal

When would The Rainbow Connection run?

The proposed service window is Saturday nights, 7:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m., with a target launch during Pride Month as a 12-week pilot. The final timetable and frequency still need to be confirmed.

Where would the bus go?

The proposed line runs between Dorothy in West Town and Whiskey Girl Tavern in Edgewater, with stops in Logan Square, Avondale, Lakeview, Northalsted, Uptown and two parts of Andersonville.

Can I ride between any two stops?

Yes. The idea is a two-way bus line, not a bar crawl. Riders would be able to board and get off at designated stops in either direction without having to visit every venue.

How often would the bus arrive?

The final frequency has not been set. The one-bus pilot schedule needs to account for travel time, stop dwell time and turnaround time at both terminals. A confirmed timetable will be shared before service begins.

How much would it cost?

The preferred goal is a fully subsidized, free-to-ride service. Funding and the final fare policy have not yet been secured.

Will I be able to track the bus?

A live tracker is something to explore with the eventual transportation operator. There is no live tracking service at this proposal stage.

Are these official bus stops?

Not yet. The route and venue locations are proposed. Final curb locations, accessibility, traffic impacts and city permissions must be reviewed before the pilot can operate.

How can my organization or business get involved?

We’re interested in hearing from venues, LGBTQ+ organizations, neighborhood groups, funders and transportation providers. Use the message builder above to prepare an introduction you can send to the project team or a potential partner.
